# Cable and connector damage

## Symptoms
ConnectorLockFailure when a driver plugs in, EVCommunicationError at session start, and sometimes GroundFailure when the insulation is damaged. Usually one connector only.

## First checks
Compare the two connectors of the charge point: if only one reports errors, the cable or the connector is the likely cause. Ask the site partner for photos before dispatching.

## On site
Inspect the CCS pins for burn marks and the cable jacket for cuts. Do not repair a liquid-cooled cable in the field; replace the cable assembly and return the old one for analysis.

## Close the ticket
Photograph the damage. Set the resolution cause to cable_damage.
