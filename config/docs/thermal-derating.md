# Thermal derating and overheating

## Symptoms
High-power charge points (150 kW and above) reduce power and then stop with HighTemperature, sometimes PowerSwitchFailure. Worse in the afternoon and in summer.

## First checks
Compare session power curves over the day. Derating that starts at the same cabinet temperature every day points to the cooling system.

## On site
Clean the air filters and check the cooling fans and the coolant level of liquid-cooled cables. Never bypass a temperature sensor.

## Close the ticket
Record the filter state before cleaning. Set the resolution cause to overheating.
