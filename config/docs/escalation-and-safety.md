# Escalation and safety

## Safety first
A charge point with visible damage, a burning smell or exposed conductors is switched off remotely and reported to the site partner immediately. Nobody works on a live high-power cabinet alone.

## When to escalate
Escalate to the vendor after two unsuccessful visits for the same fault, or when a known firmware issue has no fix. Escalate to the grid operator when several sites in one area trip at the same time.
